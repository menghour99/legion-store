The top of the line ROG Strix SCAR 17 has a 17.3-inch IPS WQHD (2,560 x 1,440 pixels) display with a 240Hz refresh rate, 300 nits brightness and 100% DCI-P3 colour gamut support. The laptop comes with the aforementioned i9-12900H CPU, an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3080Ti GPU with 150W TGP and 32GB DDR5 RAM split between two slots and a 2TB M.2 NVME PCIe 4.0 SSD with a spare slot for future upgrades. This princely set of hardware costs you RM16,999. Alternatively, you can have it with an almost identical configuration save that it uses the less powerful NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3070 Ti GPU and a 1TB SSD for RM10,000.
